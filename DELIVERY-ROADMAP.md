# Delivery roadmap

## Phase 1 — shared contract

- finalize the evidence, claim, source, period, and review schemas;
- define adapters for the six existing research repositories;
- build one shared company, industry, theme, and source identity system;
- create a source-version and refresh ledger.

## Phase 2 — one complete vertical slice

Choose one domain, preferably AI/work or infrastructure/energy, and support:

```text
signal → industry → private company → public company → social outcome
```

Produce one live theme page, company pages, a change timeline, an evidence
chain, an alert, and an exportable briefing.

## Phase 3 — quarterly intelligence

- ingest annual and quarterly reports;
- compare management claims with later results;
- track guidance, narrative changes, segment movement, cash, and capital;
- publish quarter-change briefings;
- add contradiction and promise tracking.

## Phase 4 — commercial pilot

- choose three design partners in one domain;
- give each a watchlist and recurring briefing;
- record which alerts changed a decision;
- measure source-trace use, briefing reuse, false alerts, and correction time;
- revise the product around observed work rather than assumptions.

## Phase 5 — expand the atlas

- add more domains and companies;
- expose API and export formats;
- add private customer sources with strict tenancy;
- support cross-domain comparisons only where definitions are compatible;
- build durable refresh, correction, and audit operations.

## Completion standard

The system is not complete when it has many pages or automated summaries. It
is complete when a customer can follow a meaningful change from its first signal
through industry and company response to an observed consequence, inspect every
important source, see the limits, and receive a useful next test.
