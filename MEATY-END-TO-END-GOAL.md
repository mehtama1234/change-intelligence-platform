# Meaty end-to-end goal

## The goal

Build and launch a commercially useful, continuously refreshed intelligence
platform that can follow one important change from its first visible signal to
its effects on industries, companies, institutions, and people.

The first release must prove the whole loop on one focused domain. It must not
try to cover every industry before it has demonstrated that the loop is useful.

```text
early signal or condition
→ structural pressure
→ affected people, organization, or place
→ industry mechanism
→ private and public company response
→ quarterly and operational movement
→ burden, benefit, risk, or control shift
→ measured outcome or implementation result
→ counterexample and open question
→ customer decision and next refresh
```

The product must let a customer open any meaningful statement and see the
evidence behind it, the limits of that evidence, and what could change the
reading.

## The first commercial proof

Choose one high-value domain. The recommended starting domain is **AI, work,
and enterprise control**, because it connects all six existing research
sources:

- Trend Hunter provides early signals;
- cultural and geopolitical research studies workers, households,
  institutions, and power;
- IBIS explains affected industries and operating structure;
- Inc. 5000 reveals new companies and customer pain;
- annual and quarterly reports show established-company investment and results;
- Company Atlas provides dossiers, visual explanation, and outside evidence.

The same architecture must later support infrastructure/energy, healthcare
access, affordability, and other domains. The first domain is a proof of the
system, not the permanent product boundary.

## What a customer should be able to do

A strategy or research customer should be able to:

1. choose a theme, company, industry, or question;
2. see what changed since the previous update;
3. understand the plain-English mechanism behind the change;
4. see which companies and industries are exposed;
5. compare annual structure with quarterly movement;
6. see who benefits, who pays, and who carries the work or risk;
7. inspect the exact source passage or measurement behind each claim;
8. see the strongest alternative explanation;
9. save a question, watchlist, or briefing;
10. receive a later refresh showing whether the insight held up.

## Definition of done

The research-completeness gate is executable as `npm run
verify:research-completeness`. It checks the minimum private-company, public
report-window, independent-repository, counterexample, and named-workflow
coverage below against the current packet.

The goal is complete only when all of the following are true:

### Research completeness

- one domain has a defined source map and evidence contract;
- the system contains early signals, industry records, private-company records,
  annual reports, quarterly reports, and independent outcome or implementation
  evidence;
- at least five public companies have a reproducible annual-plus-quarterly
  change history;
- at least five private-company records show customer pain and business model;
- at least three independent sources test the central domain hypothesis;
- at least one counterexample weakens or complicates the main story.
- at least one named workflow follows a defined intervention into a measured
  result, with the unmeasured correction, distribution, and durability stages
  shown rather than filled in by inference.

### Product completeness

- a reader can move from overview to company, industry, evidence, and question;
- a customer can inspect every important source trace;
- annual structure and quarterly movement are shown separately;
- the system distinguishes fact, calculation, management account,
  interpretation, and open question;
- every published insight has a next test and refresh date;
- a customer can export and reopen a source-linked briefing.

### Technical completeness

- source captures are immutable and versioned;
- every derived record has lineage to its inputs;
- refresh jobs are repeatable and observable;
- failed, partial, stale, and unavailable sources remain visible;
- tenant data and private customer sources are isolated;
- the API exposes validated read models rather than raw internal data;
- backups, recovery, access logs, and deletion procedures are tested.

### Commercial completeness

- three design partners use the product on real questions;
- each partner receives recurring updates for at least one cycle;
- at least one briefing changes a real research, strategy, product, or risk
  decision;
- false alerts and corrections are measured;
- the product has a repeatable paid offer, onboarding process, and support
  process.

## What we must not claim

The platform must never imply that:

- a trend card proves adoption;
- company growth proves customer benefit;
- a policy proves implementation;
- a permit proves operation;
- two related datasets describe the same people;
- a visual connection proves causation;
- model-generated text is independently verified;
- a large evidence count means a company is better.

## The long-term product

After the first domain works, expand by adding distinct economic systems rather
than simply adding more companies. Each new domain must use the same loop:

```text
source → observation → mechanism → affected group → consequence
→ counterexample → customer decision → refresh
```

The long-term product is a living map of change, with a reliable history of
what was believed, why it was believed, what later evidence changed, and where
uncertainty remains.
